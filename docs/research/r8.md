# r8: retrieval and the India residual

Status: paused at 13:00 UTC (usage limit); measured results below, running jobs listed in section 6.
All numbers are train-only unless marked test (label-free). Sample = the shared 10% sample (220,678 S1).

## 1. Verdict
- **Retrieval is a small lever.** The whole blocking gap is worth at most +0.0055 LB (every missed pair recovered and accepted).
  **Reverse top-1 retrieval** (+0.14 pairs per S1) plus **one new key, name prefix + house** (`p6h`) recover ~35-45% of it:
  about **+0.0015-0.002 LB** (US +0.001-0.0015, India +0.002-0.0025). Derived from measured oracle gains x measured acceptance
  of key-only pairs; medium confidence until the paired CV (section 6) finishes.
- **The India native-script residual is worth 0.** The "test-only" words are train distractor-only words that 006 already rejects.
  Do not build a mapping for them (it would remove a perfect distractor signal).

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 India native-script residual (measured, all India records, train and test)
- After the dictionary no India S1 name and no India address (train or test) keeps a native token. Residual native names exist only
  on pool records: train 64,020 (1.55% of India pool), test 121,031 (2.57%).
- They are the **same 171 tokens** in train and test (19 shop words x 9 scripts: motors, stores, agencies, jewellers, general, bakery,
  provision, sweets, pharmacy, medicals, automobiles, traders, hardware, garments, steel, electronics, furniture, restaurant, textiles).
  None is test-only; er_dict never maps them because they never occur in a gold pair.
- **Gold rate of these pool records in train: 0 of 64,020** (fully mapped native names 0.800, Latin names 0.742): pure distractors.
  006 already rejects them (max probability p99 0.0005 train OOF, 0.0003 test; none above 0.5). Test/train ratio 1.9x = the decoy excess.
- Mapping them (earlier item c) is worth 0 and would erase `nonlatin_name_p`, which currently flags them perfectly.
- Native addresses: all 17 state tokens map; 3 multi-word states map to one word (Tamil Nadu -> "nadu", Andhra Pradesh -> "andhra",
  West Bengal -> "bengal"); these are low-IDF state words, so there is no measurable lever.

### 2.2 India house-number parse noise (measured, 23,788 retrieved + 7,997 missed India gold pairs)
- 16.7% of retrieved India gold pairs have conflicting parsed house numbers; in **64% of those (10.7% of all) the S1 house is among the
  pool's numbers** (US: 8.5% conflicts, only 0.25% of this kind). This drives India's 16.8% house-conflict share on test.
- One generator form is a template plus a random number before the real one ("H.NO 930 131" vs S1 "131, Garg Plaza"). A parse fix
  (hfix.py) applies to only 1.6% of India gold pairs: house_eq 0.674 -> 0.686 (gold), 0.1475 -> 0.1495 (non-gold with p006 > 0.01),
  0.298 -> 0.327 (missed gold). The rest are multi-number ambiguities, so a symmetric "house among the other side's numbers"
  feature (r1 `h1_in_p`) is the right tool, not a parse fix.
- Name-operator vocabularies (decoy modifiers group/holdings/industries/ventures never on true matches; fillers center/services/partners
  on true matches) reproduced on the quick subset; this confirms r1/r9, whose lanes own those features.

### 2.3 Retrieval: the 2% of never-candidate gold pairs (measured, sample)
- US 132,451 S1 / 458,065 gold / 6,840 missed (1.49%), oracle 0.99549; India 88,227 / 306,209 / 7,997 (2.61%), oracle 0.99188.
  India's model loss (oracle - CV) equals the US one (0.0197); **India's extra gap vs US is entirely retrieval**.
- Missed vs retrieved gold: pool address empty US 0.468 vs 0.040, India 0.321 vs 0.032; same name_join US 0.064 vs 0.618,
  India 0.197 vs 0.740 (India misses sit in oversized same-name blocks); same house US 0.207 vs 0.788, India 0.298 vs 0.675.
- Not causes: native script (9.4% of India misses vs 17.9% of retrieved), acronyms (no miss has an initials relation).
