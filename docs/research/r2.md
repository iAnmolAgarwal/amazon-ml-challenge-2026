# r2: missed true matches (the 0.0164 bucket)

**Verdict (partial; 10% run and test guard still running):** three new feature sets aimed at the misses (H house
relation, SB source balance, HA house agreement among an S1's candidates) gain **+0.00693 on the quick subset**
(SE 0.00032, 5/5 folds; India +0.0066, US +0.0071), and a model trained on the 2% subset with them beats 006's
full-data OOF on the same S1 by +0.0052 (5/5). Guessed LB gain if the 10% run and the test guard hold: +0.003-0.006
(India and US alike, France smaller). Confidence medium-low until the guard is read: SB and HA are set-level
features, like the rejected coherence.

All numbers are measured on the shared 10% train sample (220,678 S1, 9.8M candidate pairs, 749,437 gold pairs in
candidates) unless marked. Scripts are in `~/aml/research/r2/` on A.

## 3. Signals the model does not see (measured)

- **House relation** (`hfeat.py`, pairs with p006 > 0.02): first digit dropped (801 vs 01, 2053 vs 053) is gold
  0.987 in the US at mean p 0.829 (1,654 gold missed); other suffix containment 0.948 at p 0.800; numeric
  distance <= 20 (sibling decoys) is gold 0.130 at p 0.216; transposed digits 0.099 at p 0.221. Only
  `house_prefix` exists today and leading zeros are stripped.
- **Source balance** (`gstruct.py`, `srcbal.py`): the generator splits an entity's matches between S2 and S3 far
  more evenly than chance. t=2: S3 count 0/1/2 = 4,726 / 26,000 / 5,465 (binomial 9,048 / 18,096 / 9,048);
  t=6: six S3 matches in 25 S1 (binomial 227). In the cut bucket, a candidate from a source with no emitted match
  while the other source has 2-4 is gold 0.53-0.63 at mean p 0.38-0.40; one whose source already has 4 is gold
  0.16-0.26 at p 0.33-0.35.
- **House agreement** (`hstruct.py`, `hagree.py`): house conflicts cluster in an S1 (US t=4: all four conflict
  in 163 S1 vs 1 expected; India t=5: 348 vs 2): the S1's own house was altered and its pool records agree with
  each other. Cut-bucket conflict pairs whose pool house equals an emitted sibling's: gold 0.939 (US, 4,432 pairs)
  / 0.843 (India, 3,065) at mean p 0.369 / 0.414; without agreement gold 0.09 at p 0.16-0.18.

## 4. Features and paired tests

- **H** (pairwise): `h_rel` (9 relation classes, -1 if a house is missing), `h_absd` = log(1+|h1-h2|), `h_in_p`,
  `h_in_1` (house among the other side's numbers), `n_xnum_p`, `a_tset_nonum` (address token set without numbers).
- **SB** (stage-2, like er_feat4, from `st1_p`): the S1's summed `st1_p` and count >= 0.5 over its other
  candidates from the same source and from the other source.
- **HA** (stage-2, from `st1_p`): over the S1's other candidates with the same non-empty pool house: summed
  `st1_p`, count >= 0.5, max; plus `s1_hbad` (share of the S1's candidate mass whose house conflicts with the S1).

Protocol (EC2-A load ~28 on 8 cores forced it): both arms trained identically inside the sample (harness folds)
on pairs with p006 > 0.001 (1.29M of 9.8M pairs, 749,343 of 749,437 gold); other pairs keep p006 in both arms.
LightGBM = rk.PARAMS with learning_rate 0.1, 300 rounds, 63 leaves. Decision = exact 006 rule.

Quick subset (44,136 S1), `pt2.py q`, base 0.97242 (006 on the same S1: 0.97412):

## 5. Build spec (derived)

- New side-car `src/er_feat6.py`: H from norm `house`, `nums`, `addr_norm` (code: `research/r2/hfeat.py`,
  `feats()`); ~9 min train (98M pairs) and ~8 min test single-threaded, < 4 GB in 2M-pair chunks.
- `src/er_feat4.py competition()`: add SB (group by s1 x source) and HA (group by s1 x pool house; `add_ha` in
  `research/r2/hafeat.py`); needs `is_s3`, `house_eq` and pool `house`; minutes, run in S1-aligned chunks.
- Stage 2: 006 features + H + SB + HA. Memory: all-pairs stage 2 already peaks at 61.7 GB, so train stage 2 on
  pairs with stage-1 p > 0.001 (13% of pairs, all but 0.01% of gold), as in these tests. About 2.5-3 h total on
  one 8-core/64 GB machine (feature builds 0.5 h, stage-2 CV 1 h, test 1 h), peak ~20 GB (guessed).

## 6. Risks

- SB and HA look at the S1's other candidates, like coherence. Test is 1.9x decoy-denser. HA only fires on exact
  house equality with a strong sibling, which near-number sibling decoys do not have; this is an argument, not
  evidence, until the guard is read. The guard must compare train-OOF and test shares per arm, because H and HA
  are meant to emit more house-conflict pairs.
- Gains were measured with 2% / 10% in-sample models; the full-data model is stronger, so gains may shrink.
- Mostly orthogonal to name-specificity (type C misses). France: fewer house operators and a house-parse bug
  (audit 10), so H/HA do less there; SB should transfer.
