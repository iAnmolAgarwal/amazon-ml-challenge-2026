## Setup (all measured on the shared 10% sample: 220,678 S1, 9.80M pairs, 749k positives)
- 006 stage-2 probabilities are sharply separated (measured): 8.51M pairs have p006 < 0.001 and hold 94 positives;
  the band 0.001 <= p006 < 0.999 holds 771k pairs (228k positives). Every learner or stacking effect lives in that band.
- The stage-1 band st1_p >= 0.001 (measured) keeps 14.4% of pairs and 99.991% of positives; none of the 68 lost
  positives reaches p006 > 0.5. Training stage 2 only on this band costs about 1/7 of today's training, so 80%-of-entity
  training in CV and 100% at test would cost about what 12-14% costs now. Full-train band table: 14.12M pairs, 7.49M positives.
- Protocol: 5 folds = harness folds; the decision is rk.decide with rival_out (exact pool ownership); paired rule from rk.paired.

## Stage 3 (competition features from stage-2 probabilities)
Design: stage-3 LightGBM on the 006 feature set + p006 + competition features of p006, trained in-sample (8% of
entities) on band pairs only; pairs outside the band keep p006. Arms:
- A control: 006 features + p006 (no new competition features) - separates "refit on top of p006" from stacking;
- B: + S1-side features of p006 (rank, gap to top, best other, margin, sum of others, n>=0.5, n>=0.1, gap to next and previous);
- C: + pool-side features of p006 over the full train graph (best other S1, margin, sum of others, n>=0.1 others, n S1);
- D: + decision context (kept by the 006 decision, k chosen, smallest kept p).

Leakage. S1-side features use only the S1's own pairs, all scored by the same fold model that never saw the S1's fold:
leak-free (standard out-of-fold stacking). Pool-side features (C, D) use rival S1 probabilities from other fold models,
which trained on 40% of the entities of this fold: the same indirect label path as today's stage-2 cp_* features and
the rival_out used by the decision. B vs C measures how much of any gain depends on that path. A fully leak-free
pool side is possible at 2x stage-1/2 cost: per fold, train two models on complementary halves of the other folds and
score each rival with the half-model that never saw it, so a rival probability never comes from a model that saw
either the rival or the scored fold (exactly the test situation, where no test S1 was seen).

Reading: refitting on top of p006 adds nothing (A); the whole gain comes from the S1-side competition features (B = C),
so the pool-side features, the only ones exposed to the rival-probability path, are not needed. About 60% of the
S1-side gain comes from the sum and count features (sum of the other candidates' p, n >= 0.5, n >= 0.1): B2 keeps 0.00024.

Robustness (measured): with rival=None in both arms (pool ownership only among sampled S1) C still gains +0.00061
(SE 0.00008, 5/5). By true-match count (C, 300 rounds): t=0 -0.0006 (12,320 S1), t=1 +0.0009, t=2 +0.0008,
t=3 +0.0006, t=4 +0.0006, t>=5 +0.0008. Band logloss 0.1182 -> 0.1135.

S1-level stacker (negative, measured): an out-of-fold LightGBM on 17 S1-level aggregates of p006 predicting
"no true match" beats prod(1-p) in logloss (0.0205 -> 0.0192) but not in F0.5: replacing P(no match) in the
decision gives -0.00002 (0/5 folds); the geometric mean of both gives 0.00000.
