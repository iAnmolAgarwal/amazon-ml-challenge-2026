## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 Feature groups (all label-free; statistics per split and per country from that split's S1 only)
- **NS** name specificity (10): w(t) = 1 + ln((N+1)/(df(t)+1)) over `name_core` tokens, df over the country's S1. Shared mass, S1-only and pool-only mass, their fractions, max one-sided weight on each side, IDF cosine, one-sided token counts.
- **AS** address specificity (6): the same on `addr_norm` tokens (shared, one-sided masses and maxima, cosine).
- **HS** house relations (11): dropped leading digit (`h_suffix`: one house number ends with the other), log numeric difference, small difference (≤ 10) and small difference on the same street, Levenshtein distance, digit transposition, S1 house found among the pool record's numbers and vice versa, unit/suite equal / conflict / one-sided.
- **LG** legal form (4): canonical legal-word sets from `name_lat` (pvt = private, ltd = limited, ...; website-glued suffixes included): equal, subset, disjoint, one-sided.
- **CG** (1): cosine of char-3-gram (`char_wb`) vectors of `name_join`, IDF from the country's S1.

## 3. Build spec
Two routes; both need one new side-car, `src/er_feat5.py` (a harness file, so it needs the human's approval), rebuilt from `research/r5/feat.py`:
- **Inputs:** `cache/norm/{split}_{s1,pool}.parquet` (`country_n`, `name_lat`, `name_core`, `name_join`, `addr_norm`, `house`, `street1`, `nums`) and `cache/cand/{split}_cand.parquet`. Label-free; per split and per country; IDF from the split's own S1 of that country (France gets test-France statistics).
- **Output:** `cache/feat/{split}_feat5.parquet`, 32 float32 columns keyed by (s1_idx, pool_idx), written per country in 5M-pair chunks.
- **Compute (measured on A under heavy contention, 2 threads):** 9.8M sample pairs in 172-257 s; test France 13.1M pairs in 81 s, India 37M pairs in 322 s, each under a 9 GB cap. Full train (98M pairs) is about 15-25 min per country process (derived); run US and India as two processes.

**Route 1, stage-3 corrector (cheapest, what was measured in (b)):** attempt `008s` = LightGBM 31 leaves, 300 rounds, lr 0.05 on [logit p006] + feat5, boosted from init_score = logit(OOF p006), trained on all 98M train pairs; test = model applied to test p006 + test feat5; same `decide`. OOF for the CV report: the same model 5-fold. Compute: about 5 × 8 min + 10 min on 8 threads (guessed); peak about 25 GB (98M × 33 float32 = 13 GB matrix plus LightGBM bins; derived).

**Route 2, full integration:** add feat5 to stage 1 (`007a`-style) and stage 2, retrain both, rebuild feat4. Expected to be at least as good as route 1, since the 127-leaf models can combine feat5 with the base address features (see (a)), but it costs about 4-5 h and needs the loader fix first: stage-2 CV already peaked at 61.7 of 64 GB, and +32 columns adds about 12.5 GB.

## 4. Risks and interactions
- **France legal forms (main transfer risk).** LG learns from US/India that disjoint legal forms almost never match (gold rate 0.002 / 0.000). On test France (label-free), confident 006 pairs (p006 > 0.9) have disjoint legal forms 0.75% of the time (India 0.09%), and the 0.6-0.9 band 17% (India 3.2%); examples are SARL / SAS / SASU / SA / SNC swaps on otherwise identical names. If these are noise on true French matches, LG costs France recall; if they are decoys, it helps. The test check compares ALL with ALL minus LG on France (added / dropped pairs and their house-conflict share). Fallback: drop LG for France only.
- **Decoy density on test (about 1.9x).** HS raises recall on dropped-leading-digit house numbers; the house-conflict share of emitted test pairs is the guard (section 2.4).
- **Overlap with other lanes.** Other lanes are building house (r2) and name/text (r1, r9) features; gains will not add linearly with theirs.
- **India optimism.** NS and CG use `name_core`, which relies on the Indic dictionary learned from all gold (at least 5 owners); earlier audits put this at ≤ 0.001 for India. US is unaffected.
- **In-sample rivals.** In-sample decisions compare re-scored pairs with unchanged 006 rivals (`rival_out`) for pool records also retrieved by S1 outside the sample. Both arms share this, so the effect on the paired difference should be small.
- **Stage-3 transfer.** The corrector learns on OOF p006, which comes from 40%-data fold models, while test p006 comes from the full model (sharper). A logit-additive correction should transfer, but the size of the gain on test is unmeasured.
