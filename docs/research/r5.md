## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 Feature groups (label-free; per split and per country; IDF from that split's S1 only)
- **NS** name specificity (10): w(t) = 1 + ln((N+1)/(df(t)+1)) over `name_core` tokens. Shared, S1-only and pool-only masses, their fractions, max one-sided weight on each side, IDF cosine, one-sided token counts.
- **AS** address specificity (6): the same on `addr_norm` tokens.
- **HS** house relations (11): dropped leading digit (`h_suffix`: one number ends with the other), log numeric difference, difference ≤ 10 (plus a same-street version), Levenshtein distance, digit transposition, each side's house found among the other side's numbers, unit/suite equal / conflict / one-sided.
- **LG** legal form (4): canonical legal-word sets from `name_lat` (pvt = private, ltd = limited, ...; website-glued suffixes included), classed as equal, subset, disjoint or one-sided.
- **CG** (1): IDF cosine of char-3-grams (`char_wb`) of `name_join`.

## 3. Build spec
New side-car `src/er_feat5.py` (a harness file, needs the human's approval), rebuilt from `research/r5/feat.py`. It reads norm views (`name_lat`, `name_core`, `name_join`, `addr_norm`, `house`, `street1`, `nums`) and candidates, and writes `cache/feat/{split}_feat5.parquet` (32 float32 columns, keyed by s1_idx and pool_idx) per country in 5M-pair chunks. Measured under heavy contention, 2 threads: 9.8M sample pairs in 172-257 s; test France in 81 s and India in 322 s, each under a 9 GB cap. Full train (98M pairs) takes about 15-25 min per country process (derived).
- **Route 1, stage-3 corrector (what (b) measures):** LightGBM (31 leaves, 300 rounds) on [logit p006] + feat5, init_score = logit OOF p006, trained on all train pairs. It is applied to test p006 + test feat5 with the same `decide`. About 1 h on 8 threads; peak about 25 GB (98M × 33 float32 plus bins; derived).
- **Route 2, full integration:** add feat5 to stage 1 and stage 2 and rebuild feat4. About 4-5 h. It first needs the loader fix: stage-2 CV already peaked at 61.7 of 64 GB, and feat5 adds 12.5 GB.
- Drop LG for France (or everywhere; it is worth only +0.0002) if the France guard flags it.

## 4. Risks and interactions
- **France legal forms:** see 2.4. If French legal swaps are noise on true matches, LG costs France recall; if they are decoys, it helps. The test check compares all groups with all-minus-LG per country.
- **Test decoy density (about 1.9x):** HS raises recall on dropped-digit pairs; watch the house-conflict share of emitted pairs.
- **Overlap:** other lanes are building house (r2) and name/text (r1, r9) features, so gains will not add up linearly.
- **India optimism:** `name_core` uses the Indic dictionary learned from all gold (≤ 0.001, from earlier audits). US is unaffected.
- **Stage-3 transfer:** trained on OOF p006 (40%-data folds); test p006 comes from the full model and is sharper. A logit-additive correction should transfer, but the size of the test gain is unmeasured.
