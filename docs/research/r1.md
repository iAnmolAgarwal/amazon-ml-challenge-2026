# r1: generator forensics (decoys vs true noise)

Status 2026-09-25 ~13:00 UTC: the forensics are complete and measured. The paired CV and the test guard are still queued on A (see "Pending when resumed").

## 1. Verdict
Decoys and true-match noise come from different, nearly disjoint operators, and 006 cannot see them. Examples: a moved house number
(+-1..30) vs a truncated one; inserted industry or location words vs "center/services" filler; a changed legal form; a changed floor or
unit number. Expected gain (guessed from the loss buckets they target, not yet measured): +0.002 to +0.004 CV for India and US; France
about 0 from the vocabulary features unless the label-free group R is used.
Confidence in the operator separation: high (likelihood ratios 0.04-51 on 15-140k pairs). Confidence in the gain: pending.

### 2.2 House-number operators (quick subset; house conflict and n_tset >= 80: 15,809 gold vs 65,298 unowned)
Gold rate / decoy rate (LR): first digit dropped 11.3% / 0.22% (51); last digit dropped 14.0% / 0.69% (20); two digits dropped LR 8-21;
one non-last digit changed 4.6% / 0.42% (11). Decoy side: last digit changed 11.0% / 18.6% (0.59); |delta| <= 10 2.7% / 11.5% (0.23);
|delta| <= 30 4.6% / 14.0% (0.33); transposition LR 0.32. 006 only has house_eq and a startswith prefix flag.
A conflicting number repeated by another candidate of the same S1 with stage-1 p > 0.5: gold 64.0%, unowned 0.37%, other-S1 0.22%;
in the band 0.05 < p006 < 0.95: 58.8% vs 1.45%. Per-source versions of an entity carry the same altered number (a gold conflict shares
its number with another gold record 67.6% of the time; a decoy shares it with another decoy only 7.0%).

### 2.3 Name operators (quick subset; n_tset >= 60: 140,869 gold vs 94,810 unowned)
- Pure insertion: 4.7% vs 33.4% (LR 0.14); identical token sets: 70.8% vs 32.0%; two new words for one dropped word: LR 0.04.
- Legal form changed (PLLC to LLC, Private Limited to LLP): 4.5% vs 25.0% (0.18); legal word added: LR 0.32.
- Inserted words per 10k pairs, gold vs unowned: group 0/543, holdings 0/498, industries 0/129, ventures 0/119, public 0/113,
  exports 0/107, overseas 0/106, infratech 0/100, and north/east/metro/uptown/midtown/downtown/lakeside/harbor/riverside ~0/65 each.
  True-match filler: center 290/166, services 197/111, service 102/42, plus alias markers. The out-of-sample table (hash 100-199,
  3.0M pairs) confirms 112 decoy-only words. token_set_ratio scores both kinds of insertion as 100.
- Typo shape (pairs differing by exactly one typo token: gold 66 vs unowned 18 per 1k pairs): true-match typos are 2-3 edits inside
  the token (LR 6-7) or digit-for-letter (LR 11-20). Decoy "typos" edit the token's end (1 edit LR 0.80, 2 edits LR 0.29): Lumelle to
  Lumello, ZS to ZST. Not built as a feature yet.

### 2.4 Address operators when the house number is equal (quick subset; 97,602 gold vs 5,978 unowned)
The pool address carries a number absent from the S1 address: gold 7.4% vs unowned 63.8% (LR 0.12). Decoys change a floor, unit or
sub-number (12/615(1) to 12/615(5)); true matches drop numbers. Decoys also add locality words (road, floor, nagar, sector:
13-45 per 1k vs about 0); true matches add state names and codes, "null", PO box.

### 2.5 Empty pool address (the +0.0049 FN bucket) is mostly irreducible (quick subset, n_tset >= 90)
Gold rate by the number of S1 carrying the pool's exact name: 0: 3.9% (24,700 pairs); 1: 23.9% (mean p006 0.238, calibrated);
2-11: 2-10%. Missed gold with p006 <= 0.1 share their name with 49 S1 on average and lose to a rival 94% of the time. Not pursued.

### 2.6 Test, label-free (full norm files, f9.py)
- The 112 decoy-only words, pool occurrences per S1: train India 2.27, US 2.57; test India 3.11 (+37%), US 3.59 (+40%). Unchanged

### 2.7 Leader hypothesis: decoy deviations repeat across pool records (quick subset)

House deviations go the opposite way (2.2). Verdict: weak for names. The end-of-token decoy edit in 2.3 is the part that holds.

## 3. Feature groups (code: research/r1/r1lib.py, r1_feat.py, r1_feat2.py, r1_feat3.py)
- H, house relation (pairwise, label-free): operator code, log|delta|, length difference, position of the changed digit, and each side's
  house number found among the other side's numbers.
- N, name operators (pairwise): fuzzy-aligned inserted, dropped and typo counts; worst typo ratio; legal-form change code; inserted- and
  dropped-word LR (min/max/sum) from the out-of-sample table (no sampled S1 label used); unknown-word count.
- S, house support (stage-2 style, from st1_p): other candidates sharing the pool house number (any; st1_p > 0.5; same source), S1-house support.
- A, secondary numbers (pairwise, label-free): new and dropped numbers excluding the houses, new numbers within +-30 of an S1 number, truncations.
- R, France-capable vocabulary (label-free, per split and country): log over-representation of each inserted word in pool vs S1 names (max, sum, count > 3).

## 4. Build spec
- New side-car `src/er_feat6.py` (H, A, N; R needs only split-level token counts, like er_feat2's name frequencies). Port hrel/align/
  legal_code/pair_rows. Measured about 40k pairs/s on 3 processes, so about 25 min for 98M train and 20 min for 79M test on 8 cores; about 6 GB per 4M batch.
- N's word table must be per fold for train OOF (built from the other folds' S1) and from all of train for test (a new step in er_dict.py).
- S goes next to er_feat4 (it needs stage-1 probabilities): window counts, minutes.
- Order: stage-1 with H+N+A(+R), then feat4 + S from the new stage-1 OOF, then stage-2. Memory: +20-32 float32 columns on 98M pairs
  (+8-12 GB), on top of 006's 61.7 GB peak. The loader fix in STATUS (gather by key, no frame copy) comes first, or train on 50% of the S1.

## 5. Risks and interactions
- S is the only cross-candidate group (coherence-trap risk on the decoy-denser test). H, N, A and R are pairwise.
- The guard: recovered truncated-number true matches are house conflicts, so judge the emitted house-conflict change against the same
  change on train OOF, and together with the nearby-number share (the decoy signature), which should fall.
- Overlaps name-specificity (er_feat5): rare pool-only tokens are partly decoy words, so the gains will not add fully.
- `ins_nf` uses a 17-word filler list read off quick-subset forensics; a clean build derives it per fold.
