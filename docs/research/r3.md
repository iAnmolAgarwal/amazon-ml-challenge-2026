### 2.1 Label-free self-estimate of F0.5 (006 probabilities, Monte Carlo over pair probabilities, 16 draws)
The self-estimate is the F0.5 the model expects under its own probabilities, using the real decision rule. It cannot see blocking misses.

| | S1 | emitted / S1 | empty | self-estimated F0.5 | actual F0.5 | pairs per S1 with p in (0.05, 0.95] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| train sample US (OOF p006) | 132,451 | | 5.97% | 0.97841 | 0.97588 | |
| train sample India (OOF p006) | 88,227 | | 5.93% | 0.97885 | 0.97225 | |
| test US | 663,106 | 3.329 | 5.89% | 0.97316 | ? | 0.937 |
| test India | 809,986 | 3.316 | 5.85% | 0.97304 | ? | 0.900 |
| test France | 259,452 | 3.448 | 4.99% | 0.96684 | ? | 1.212 |

Measured. Test self-estimates sit 0.005-0.006 below train for US and India, and France sits another 0.006 lower. The test probabilities come from the full-data model, which is sharper than OOF, so this gap is not an artifact of sharpness.

### 2.3 France is a structural shift: street agreement stops being evidence (label-free)
Stage-1 feature means. Train = quick sample, split by label. France = 20k random test S1, split by whether 006 emitted the pair.

| feature | US neg | US pos | India neg | India pos | France not emitted | France emitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| street_eq | 0.150 | 0.842 | 0.204 | 0.639 | **0.775** | 0.824 |
| a_tset | 60.8 | 90.0 | 71.1 | 93.1 | **83.9** | 92.4 |
| a_ratio_g | 55.4 | 79.3 | 57.7 | 77.5 | **73.9** | 76.0 |
| word_sim | 0.255 | 0.663 | 0.250 | 0.726 | **0.399** | 0.739 |
| house_eq | -0.18 | 0.66 | -0.01 | 0.51 | 0.06 | 0.65 |
| num_jacc | 0.09 | 0.74 | 0.13 | 0.68 | 0.20 | 0.89 |
| n_tset (name) | 48.9 | 93.1 | 41.5 | 93.8 | 45.0 | 92.0 |
| postal_eq | -0.999 | -0.996 | -1.000 | -0.997 | -0.999 | -0.995 |

Measured.
- In training, street agreement separates matches from non-matches (0.84 vs 0.15 in US). In France, 77.5% of the candidates the model rejects share the S1's street, so address agreement carries little evidence there. Only the house number and the name discriminate.
- Postal features are dead in every country (`postal_eq` = -1 on 99.9% of pairs), so they are not a France-specific gap.

**Pool-side over-claiming**, a lower bound on overconfidence: a pool record matches at most one S1, so Σ over S1 of p should be ≤ 1. Measured on 006 probabilities, full train OOF and full test:

| | excess mass Σmax(0, Σp−1) per S1 | pool records claimed by ≥ 2 S1 at p > 0.2, per S1 | total p mass per S1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| train US / India | 0.0028 / 0.0015 | 0.035 / 0.024 | 3.40 / 3.38 |
| test US / India | 0.0021 / 0.0014 | 0.029 / 0.024 | 3.52 / 3.49 |
| **test France** | **0.0090** | **0.056** | **3.68** |

France over-claims 4.4x as much as US test and 6.5x as much as India test, and carries 0.16-0.19 more probability mass per S1. This is label-free evidence of overconfidence specific to France. It is consistent with look-alike candidates on the same street, which a model trained on US/India does not expect.

### 2.4 What the French gray zone looks like (30 random France S1 with a borderline pair, `frsample.py`)
The dominant confusion is a **sibling decoy on the same street in the same town, a few house numbers away, with a near-identical name**. The name usually differs only in legal form, which name normalization removes.
- S1 'Team Travaux SARL', 41 Rue de Phalsbourg, Calais: emitted 'Team Tràvaux SA' at **42** R. de Phalsbourg (p 0.915). Other records at 42 ('Team Travaux S.C.I.', 'team sante sarl') form a separate cluster.
- S1 'Confrerie Centre SARL', 370 Cours de la Somme: emitted 'Confrerie Centre Sa' at '**375** Crs De La Somre' (p 0.917). The pool house was **dropped by the parser** ("Crs" is not in `FR_STREET`), so the conflict was invisible to the model.
- The same pattern sits just below the floor: 'Maison Socio SAS' at 52 vs 47 (p 0.505), 'Gardes Loisirs SARL' at 34 vs 23 (0.514), 'Indus Union Eurl' at 4 A vs 3 A (0.390).

Same-street candidates in these samples are almost all in the S1's own town. Different-town cases exist but are rare (Lège-Cap-Ferret vs La Teste-de-Buch at p 0.46; Calais vs Nantes at 0.38); `city.py` quantifies this.

In France the house number is the only address field that discriminates, and French true matches show almost no US-style house noise. The 10_audit prefix/suffix share is 0.2% of France emitted pairs vs 4.3% of US gold. So a French house conflict is probably a decoy, while the model learned in US/India that conflicts are often matches.
