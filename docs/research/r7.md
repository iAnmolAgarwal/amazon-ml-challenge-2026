# r7: graph structure among pool records (decoy-robust)

Status: paused by the usage limit. Diagnostics are measured; the paired experiments are queued or running on A (see 6).

## 1. Verdict (provisional)
- **Graph angle: negative, with direct evidence (measured, label-free on test).**
  - Test decoys come in clusters that share their perturbed house number; train decoys do not (2.4).
  - So pool-pool coherence, support, clustering, component assignment and transitive rescue all learn "shared deviation = true" on train and would emit test decoy clusters. This is the mechanism behind the old coherence failure. Do not build them.
- **Pivot: judge each pair against the S1 alone, with a house-edit signature.**
  - Decoys shift the house number by a small edit: 634 -> 637, 4101 -> 4112.
  - The 006 features cannot see this (2.3).
  - Upper bound (derived): ~0.0018 US CV and ~0.007 US test. Expected LB gain if the queued runs pass: +0.001 to +0.003 (US, India). Confidence low until exp1/exp3 and the test guard finish.

## 2. Evidence

### 2.1 What the decoys are (train, labels; 10% sample, 006 OOF decision) - measured
- Emitted false positives: 4,375 are orphans (pool records owned by no S1) and 474 belong to another S1. Orphans are 90% of false positives.
- House-conflict share of emitted pairs: orphan false positives US 51% / India 35%; true positives 6.8% / 16.0%.
- Decoys are near-copies of the S1's entity with one perturbed field (house shifted, name token mutated, suffix swapped). Sometimes they copy a true record's address typos exactly.
- In train, decoys are singletons. Orphans with p006 > 0.05 per S1: 469k S1 have 1, 16k have 2, 0.7k have 3 or more.
- Missed gold pairs (45,851 of 764,274): low probability 16.6k, not retrieved 14.8k, lost to a rival 8.8k, top-k cut 5.6k.

### 2.2 What differs on test (label-free) - measured
- Test's ~1.1 extra pool records per S1 fall mostly in the 0.001-0.9 best-probability range.
  - US: 0.01-0.1 +0.44, 0.1-0.3 +0.12, 0.3-0.5 +0.047, 0.5-0.7 +0.026.
  - The unretrieved and < 0.001 buckets add only +0.10 (US) / +0.32 (India).
  - Implied orphan rate in 0.1-0.9: 2.3-2.8x train.
- So the excess is near-duplicate decoys of existing S1, not orphan clusters from missing S1.
- US house-conflict candidates per S1 roughly double (0.05-0.3: 0.179 -> 0.403; 0.3-0.6: 0.056 -> 0.118); house-equal ones barely move.
- Script: `eda3.py`, `eda4.py`.

### 2.3 Decoy signature: small house edits - measured
House-conflict pairs with p006 > 0.05, train precision by edit kind:

| edit kind | US | India | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 digit substituted | 0.40 | 0.73 | US orphans 10,025 |
| edit distance 2 | 0.23 | 0.73 | US orphans 13,128 |
| edit distance >= 3 (e.g. 730 vs 335) | 0.80 | 0.91 | |
| substring (1320 vs 132) | 0.92 | 0.88 | |

- 006 has house_eq, house_prefix and num_jacc, which are 0 for both 634/637 and 730/335.
- Edit distance alone mixes the two (730 vs 335 has distance 2), hence the numeric-difference features h_nabs / h_nrel in 2.5.
- Test / train per S1, US: 1-substitution and edit-distance-2 rise 2.3-2.6x in the 0.05-0.6 bands. Edit distance >= 3 and substrings rise 1.1-1.6x.
- Implied test precision (own density fixed) in the 0.6-0.9 band:
  - US: 1-substitution 0.40 (train 0.70), edit distance 2 0.23 (train 0.54);
  - India: 0.56 (0.79) and 0.41 (0.65).
- Above 0.9 (US): 0.87 (0.97) and 0.82 (0.95).
- Derived upper bound, if every pair above 0.6 were emitted: small-edit false positives per US S1 are 0.0098 on train and 0.0375 on test. At ~0.19 F0.5 each, that is ~0.0018 of US CV loss and ~0.007 of US test loss. Rejecting them is worth ~4x more on test than on CV.
- Script: `eda8.py`.

### 2.4 Test decoys cluster; train decoys do not - measured (the leader's hypothesis holds)
- Shared house deviation = a near candidate (p006 > 0.05) whose house differs from the S1's and equals another near candidate's.
- Train: 96% of these are true matches (the entity's second address): US 22,968 own vs 882 orphans; India 35,884 vs 508.
- Per S1, train -> test:

| band | US | India |
|---|---|---|
| 0.05-0.3 | 0.019 -> 0.184 (9.6x) | 0.013 -> 0.085 (6.6x) |
| 0.3-0.6 | 0.017 -> 0.070 (4.3x) | 0.015 -> 0.036 (2.4x) |
| 0.6-0.9 | 0.024 -> 0.045 (1.9x) | 0.034 -> 0.045 (1.3x) |
| > 0.9 | 0.121 -> 0.133 | 0.353 -> 0.370 |

- Candidates with a unique deviating name token rise only ~2x.
- House support (another p > 0.5 candidate with the same house): train orphans with a conflict 1% (US) / 4% (India) vs true matches 57% / 71%. The implied support rate of test's extra decoys is ~15%.
- A model given sibling agreement, including a "unique deviation" flag (0 means shared), promotes these clusters on test. Train has too few decoy clusters to learn the reverse sign.
- Script: `eda5.py`, `eda4.py`.

### 2.5 Paired experiments - designed, queued (results pending, see 6)
Arms, 5-fold in-sample LightGBM (same folds, 300 rounds, lr 0.1, identical for all arms):
- base: 006 set.
- hed: base + h_lev, h_1sub, h_sub, h_ldiff, h_nabs, h_nrel.
- gneg: base + g_huniq, g_atwin_hi, g_ntwin_hi.
- gall: gneg + support and shared-deviation counts.

Conditions (models trained on the normal sample):
- normal;
- clone_ind: each near orphan duplicated once, with its features;
- clone_dep: clones share house, name and address with the orphan (decoy clusters, 2.4);
- removal: 19% of all S1 removed; n_rival, rev_rank/gap, cp_* and the rival recomputed without them.

Also reported: a decoy-weighted paired diff (S1 with near orphans weighted 2.4).

Test guard: base vs hed trained on the quick subset. Reports emitted per S1, empty rate, house-conflict share, and small-edit share per country.

## 3. Build spec (if hed passes)
- New side-car `src/er_feat6.py`: house-edit features for every candidate pair from norm `house` (S1 vs pool). rapidfuzz Levenshtein runs only where both are non-empty and differ; numeric difference only for all-digit houses.
- Add the features to stage 1 and stage 2, then rerun the 006 chain.

- Follow-up idea (untested): an anchored S1-side count (strong candidates at the S1's exact house) as context for the small-edit flag. Decoy clusters cannot raise it.

## 4. Risks and interactions
- The house-edit features may overlap another lane's house features. A combined run must check that the gains add.
- France is unlabeled. The small-edit penalty transfers untested; test France small-edit pairs are 0.02-0.16 per S1 in the low bands.
- Transitive rescue through exact address twins is unsafe: name-mutated decoys copy a true record's address, typos included (Olanoari / Novinovinyla share "south joordan").
- The removal simulation changes little by design: the test excess is not missing S1 (2.2). clone_dep is the relevant stress test.
